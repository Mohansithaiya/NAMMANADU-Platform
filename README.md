# NAMMANADU Platform

> **AI-powered citizen governance platform for smarter public services, complaint management, emergency response, and digital governance.**

NAMMANADU is a full-stack civic technology platform designed to make it easier for citizens to interact with public services, report civic issues, track complaints, and access government-related assistance through a modern digital interface.

The platform focuses on building a transparent and accessible bridge between **citizens, government services, and digital governance systems.**

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

Citizens often face difficulties when reporting civic problems, understanding government schemes, and tracking the progress of their complaints.

**NAMMANADU** aims to address these challenges through a centralized platform that provides:

- 📝 Digital complaint submission
- 📍 Location-based civic issue reporting
- 📷 Complaint evidence / image attachments
- 🔎 Complaint tracking
- 📊 Citizen complaint dashboard
- 🤖 AI-assisted civic assistance
- 🏛️ Government scheme assistance
- 🚨 Emergency response capabilities
- 👥 Role-based access
- 🔐 Secure authentication

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👤 Citizen Services

Citizens can:

- Create an account and securely log in
- Access a personalized citizen dashboard
- Submit civic complaints
- Provide complaint descriptions and categories
- Select complaint priority
- Provide district and constituency information
- Track submitted complaints
- View complaint status
- Search and filter complaints
- View complaint statistics
- Upload supporting evidence/images
